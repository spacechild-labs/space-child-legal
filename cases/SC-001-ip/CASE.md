# SC-001: Space Child IP Protection & Licensing

**Status:** Active  
**Priority:** High  
**Opened:** 2026-03-01

## Objective

Protect the Space Child ecosystem's intellectual property and create a novel license — the **Space Child License** — that is legally credible and aligned with the mission.

## The Space Child License — Design Requirements

### Core Philosophy
- **Free for peaceful, humanitarian use** — encouraged, not just permitted
- **Violation = use in warfare, oppression, or harm against humanity**
- **Government/military**: self-defense permitted; offensive/war operations require paid licensing
- **Alignment**: Anthropic's safety ethos + OpenAI's government engagement reality
- **Legally credible**: not aspirational, enforceable

### Key Provisions
1. **Grant**: Free, worldwide, perpetual license for peaceful human-benefiting purposes
2. **Peace Clause**: Use in armed aggression, weapons systems targeting civilians, oppression of populations = violation
3. **Self-Defense Carve-out**: Defensive military/government use permitted (reality accepted)
4. **War Tax**: Department of War / offensive military operations must negotiate paid license
5. **Attribution**: Required
6. **Modification**: Permitted under same license terms (copyleft for the peace clause)
7. **Commercial Use**: Permitted for peaceful commercial purposes (no restriction)
8. **Enforcement**: Violations terminate license; injunctive relief available
