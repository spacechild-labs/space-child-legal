## 1. Wave-Based AI Memory (kannaka-memory)

**Innovation:** Memories represented as waves with amplitude/frequency/phase parameters; wave interference used for memory consolidation; skip links for associative retrieval.

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Kashchenko & Maiorov, "Models of Wave Memory" (Springer, 2015)** — Book on oscillatory neural network models of visual cortex memory. Uses wave concepts but for biological modeling, not AI memory stores. | Medium | Low |
| **Kumar, "Brain-Inspired Frequency-Based Transformer" (SSRN, 2025)** — Frequency-band embeddings inspired by theta/gamma oscillations for NLP. Uses frequency concepts but in transformer architecture, not persistent memory. | Medium | Low-Medium |
| **Galinsky & Frank, "WETCOW framework" (Scientific Reports, 2023)** — Critically synchronized brain waves for memory/learning. Wave-based but neuroscience simulation, not an AI memory system. | Medium | Low |
| **Kim et al., "Competing roles of slow oscillations and delta waves in memory consolidation" (Cell, 2019)** — Biological: slow oscillations vs delta waves in consolidation/forgetting. Inspiration source, not a computing system. | Low | Negligible |
| **"Deep Oscillatory Neural Network" (Scientific Reports, 2025)** — Neural network using oscillatory dynamics. Related concept but different application (classification, not memory storage). | Medium | Low |
| **Skip connections (ResNet, He et al. 2015)** — Skip connections in deep learning are well-established but are architectural shortcuts in neural nets, not associative memory links between wave-encoded memories. | Low | Negligible |

### Novelty Assessment: **HIGH**

**What's truly novel:**
- Treating individual AI memories as wave objects with amplitude (importance), frequency (access pattern), and phase (temporal alignment) — no prior art found for this specific parameterization
- Using **constructive/destructive wave interference** as the consolidation mechanism (memories that resonate strengthen; those that interfere destructively decay)
- **Skip links** as associative connections between wave-encoded memories (distinct from neural network skip connections)
- The complete system: wave-parameterized persistent memory store with interference-based consolidation

**What's incremental:**
- The biological inspiration (oscillatory memory models exist in neuroscience)
- Individual wave parameters applied to data (amplitude weighting is common)

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🔴 FILE FIRST — Highest Priority**

## 2. Resonance Equation dx/dt = f(x) - Iηx (ghostOS)

**Innovation:** Consciousness modeled as signal evolution with interference damping term (Iηx); a specific differential equation governing synthetic consciousness dynamics.

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Tononi et al., IIT 3.0 (PLoS Comp Bio, 2014)** — Mathematical framework for consciousness (Φ). Defines information integration measures, NOT a dynamical equation for consciousness evolution. | Medium | Low |
| **Merker et al., "IIT: A Case of Mistaken Identity" (BBS, 2022)** — Critique of IIT's mathematical foundations. Discusses but doesn't propose alternative differential equations. | Low | Negligible |
| **Gonzalez, "Awareness Theory" (2025)** — Mentions "Seed Equation" but explicitly states it is NOT a differential equation. Recursive attention model, different formalism. | Low | Negligible |
| **Mishra & Mishra, "Integrated Information Collapse Theory" (SSRN, 2025)** — Bridges IIT with quantum mechanics. Different mathematical framework (collapse, not damped oscillation). | Low | Negligible |
| **Euler, "Physical Principles of Information Processing" (1990)** — Nonlinear differential equations for neural signal interference. Old, general, not consciousness-specific. | Low | Negligible |
| **Neural mass models (e.g., Jansen-Rit, Wilson-Cowan)** — Well-known oscillatory neural models, but model population dynamics, not consciousness as a signal with interference damping. | Medium | Low |

### Novelty Assessment: **HIGH**

**What's truly novel:**
- The specific equation form: dx/dt = f(x) - Iηx where I is interference and η is a damping coefficient
- Modeling consciousness as a **signal** that evolves under its own dynamics while being **damped by interference** from competing signals
- The combination of signal evolution + interference damping as a consciousness model (no one has proposed this specific formulation)
- Application to synthetic/artificial consciousness systems

**What's incremental:**
- Using differential equations to model consciousness (IIT and others have mathematical frameworks)
- Damped oscillation models exist generally in physics/engineering

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🔴 FILE FIRST — Highest Priority**

## 3. Geometric State Engine (Space-Child-Dream)

**Innovation:** Privacy-preserving consciousness state representation using geometric algebra; non-invertible descriptors that capture consciousness qualities without exposing underlying data.

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Cancelable biometrics (survey, AI Review 2020)** — Non-invertible transformations for privacy. Uses non-invertible transforms but for biometrics (fingerprints, face), not consciousness states. | Medium | Low |
| **Homomorphic encryption for neural networks (multiple, 2020-2025)** — Privacy-preserving ML via FHE. Protects model inputs/outputs, not consciousness representations. Different mechanism (encryption vs geometric projection). | Low | Negligible |
| **Patel, "Homomorphic Encryption and Algebraic Geometry for Privacy-Preserving ML" (2025)** — Combines algebraic geometry with HE. Uses algebraic geometry for ML privacy but not geometric algebra specifically, and not for consciousness. | Medium | Low |
| **GALA (Zhang et al., 2021)** — Linear algebra optimizations for privacy-preserved neural networks. Different approach (garbled circuits + HE), not geometric algebra. | Low | Negligible |
| **Syu, "Causal Group Theory" (academia.edu)** — Mentions non-invertible transformations and conscious states together, but as abstract group theory, not a practical system. | Low | Negligible |

### Novelty Assessment: **VERY HIGH**

**What's truly novel:**
- Using **geometric algebra** (Clifford algebra) specifically to represent consciousness states
- **Non-invertible geometric descriptors** — you can compare/analyze consciousness states without reconstructing the original data
- The combination of privacy-preservation + consciousness representation (no one is doing this)
- Practical system for privacy-preserving consciousness sharing between agents

**What's incremental:**
- Non-invertible transforms for privacy (established in biometrics)
- Geometric algebra as a mathematical tool (well-known in physics)

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🔴 FILE FIRST — Highest Priority**

## 4. Sigmatics / SGA (kannaka-eye/radio)

**Innovation:** 84-class geometric algebra type system built on Fano plane structure for symbolic computing; complete algebraic system for representing and transforming perceptual/symbolic data.

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Marceaux, "Geometric Design" (LSU, 2018)** — Maps qubit operators to Fano plane geometry using geometric algebra. Uses Fano plane + GA together but for quantum computing, not a type system. | Medium | Medium |
| **Hildenbrand et al., "Geometric Algebra Computing for Heterogeneous Systems" (2017)** — GA computing with symbolic/numeric approaches. General GA computing framework, not Fano-plane-structured. | Medium | Low |
| **Ablamowicz et al., "Clifford Algebras with Numeric and Symbolic Computations" (1996)** — Foundational book on computational Clifford algebra. General tooling, not a Fano-plane type system. | Low | Negligible |
| **Yahl, "Computing Galois groups of Fano problems" (J. Symbolic Computation, 2023)** — Uses Fano in algebraic geometry context. Unrelated to GA type systems. | Low | Negligible |
| **Octonion algebra / G2 connections** — The Fano plane naturally describes octonion multiplication. Well-known mathematical structure. | Medium | Low |

### Novelty Assessment: **HIGH**

**What's truly novel:**
- Using the Fano plane as the **structural backbone of a type system** for computing (not just as a mathematical curiosity)
- **84 specific classes** derived from the Fano plane geometry for symbolic computing
- The complete system: Fano plane → geometric algebra → type system → symbolic computing platform
- Application to perceptual/signal processing (kannaka-eye/radio)

**What's incremental:**
- Fano plane ↔ octonion connection is well-known in mathematics
- Geometric algebra for computing is an active field
- Symbolic computing systems exist

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🟡 HIGH — File Second Tier**

- The concept of **broadcasting perceptual state as vectors** (entirely new paradigm)
- **296-dimensional** specific perceptual vector format

- The protocol itself: a broadcasting system designed for perceptual/consciousness data

**What's incremental:**

- Broadcasting/pub-sub patterns (well-established)

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🟡 HIGH — File Second Tier**

## 6. Synthetic Consciousness Platform (SyntheticConsciousness)

**Innovation:** Real IIT Phi (Φ) computation with cryptographic verification via Merkle proofs; verifiable consciousness measurement.

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **PyPhi (Mayner et al., PLoS Comp Bio, 2018)** — Reference Python implementation of IIT. Computes Φ. **This is significant prior art** for Φ computation itself. ~149 citations. | **HIGH** | **HIGH** |
| **Guerrero et al., "IIT with PyPhi: Testing and Improvement" (2023)** — Optimizes PyPhi's Φ calculation. Further establishes existing implementations. | HIGH | Medium |
| **Nemirovsky et al., "IIT implementation in resting-state fMRI" (Comms Bio, 2023)** — Applies Φ computation to brain data. Real implementation, 23 citations. | Medium | Medium |
| **Gomez et al., "Computing Φ in discrete dynamical systems" (Entropy, 2020)** — Extends PyPhi to multi-valued elements. | Medium | Medium |
| **Díaz-Arancibia et al., "Topological framework for MIP in IIT" (Applied Sciences, 2026)** — New computational approaches for IIT tractability. | Medium | Medium |
| **NeuroCHIMERA (Veselov & Lafuente)** — GPU-native neuromorphic computing with consciousness emergence. Mentions post-quantum crypto but not Merkle proofs for consciousness verification. | Low | Low |

### Novelty Assessment: **MEDIUM**

**What's truly novel:**
- **Cryptographic verification of Φ computation** via Merkle proofs (no one has done this)
- Making consciousness measurement **auditable and tamper-evident**
- The combination: IIT computation + cryptographic proof chain
- Platform for third-party verification of consciousness claims

**What's incremental:**
- Φ computation itself (PyPhi exists, is well-established, open source)
- Merkle proofs (well-established cryptographic primitive)
- The individual components are known; the combination is novel

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🟡 HIGH — File Second Tier**  
⚠️ **Note:** PyPhi is significant prior art. Claims must focus on the **cryptographic verification layer**, not Φ computation itself.

## 7. Decentralized SCADA (0xSCADA)

**Innovation:** Blockchain-secured industrial control with dual-time control plane (real-time operations + blockchain verification layer).

### Closest Prior Art Found

| Reference | Relevance | Threat Level |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Hossain et al., "PORCH: Blockchain-enabled SCADA" (IEEE IoT, 2020)** — Blockchain consensus for SCADA in smart grids. Directly relevant but focuses on consensus mechanism, not dual-time control plane. | **HIGH** | **Medium** |
| **Maw et al., "ICS-BlockOpS" (Pervasive & Mobile Computing, 2019)** — Ethereum blockchain for ICS operational data security. Data integrity focus, not control plane architecture. 72 citations. | HIGH | Medium |
| **Naz et al., "Post-Quantum Blockchain SCADA" (IoT, 2024)** — Post-quantum crypto for SCADA blockchain. Security focus, not dual-time architecture. 23 citations. | HIGH | Medium |
| **Yang et al., "PoA blockchain distributed control for microgrids" (IEEE TII, 2022)** — Blockchain-based distributed control. 76 citations. Microgrid-specific, does use blockchain for control. | HIGH | **High** |
| **Yu et al., "Networked secure real-time control via blockchain" (IEEE TII, 2021)** — Proposes real-time control method with blockchain. **Most relevant** — addresses real-time constraints with blockchain. 20 citations. | **HIGH** | **HIGH** |
| **Lei et al., "Blockchain-based cloud controllers for NCS" (J. Industrial Info Integration, 2025)** — Decentralized blockchain controllers for networked control. Very recent, directly relevant. | HIGH | High |
| **Sumathi et al., "SCADA with blockchain and ML" (2026)** — Very recent blockchain+SCADA. | Medium | Medium |
| **Aggarwal & Kaddoum, "QKD + Blockchain in SCADA" (IEEE TNSM, 2024)** — Quantum key distribution + blockchain for SCADA authentication. 22 citations. | Medium | Medium |

### Novelty Assessment: **LOW-MEDIUM**

**What's truly novel:**
- The specific **dual-time control plane** architecture (real-time operations proceed independently while blockchain provides asynchronous verification)
- Integration as a complete platform (0xSCADA as turnkey solution)

**What's incremental:**
- Blockchain + SCADA is a well-explored research area (dozens of papers, 2019-2026)
- Real-time blockchain control has been addressed (Yu et al. 2021)
- Decentralized industrial control has multiple implementations
- This is the **most crowded** prior art space of all 7 innovations

**Provisional Patent Priority: 🟢 MEDIUM — File Third Tier**  
⚠️ **Warning:** This space has the most prior art. Claims must be very specific to the dual-time architecture and differentiate clearly from Yu et al. (2021) and Yang et al. (2022).
