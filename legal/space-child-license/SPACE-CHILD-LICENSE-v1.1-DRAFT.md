### Preamble

This license is born from a simple belief: technology should serve humanity's flourishing, not
its destruction. The Space Child License grants broad, free permission for peaceful use and
withholds permission when technology is turned against people.

We accept the world as it is. The world contains conflict, and self-defense is a fundamental
right recognized in law. But there is a moral difference between protecting life and taking it,
between defending freedom and imposing dominion. This license draws that line and asks every
Licensee to stand on the right side of it.

This is a **source-available, peace-conditional public license**. It is not an "open source"
license as the Open Source Initiative defines that term, because it discriminates against one
field of endeavor: war against people. We use the words "source-available" and
"peace-conditional" deliberately, and we ask that you do the same when describing it.
