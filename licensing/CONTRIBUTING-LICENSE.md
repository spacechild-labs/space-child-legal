# Contributing under the Space Child License

Paste this section into a repository's `CONTRIBUTING.md` (or use it as the whole file for a small
project). It states the inbound terms so that every contribution arrives under the same license the
project is offered under, and so that the Licensor can later grant the separate agreements the
license provides for.

---

## License of contributions

This project is offered under the [Space Child License v1.0](https://legal.spacechild.love/license),
a source-available, peace-conditional license. By submitting a contribution — a pull request, a
patch, a file, a design — you agree that:

1. **Inbound = outbound.** Your contribution is licensed to the project and to everyone who receives
   it under the Space Child License, the same terms as the rest of the project, with no additional
   terms of your own.
2. **You have the right to submit it.** It is your original work, or you have permission from its
   owner (including your employer, if the work was done on their time or equipment) to submit it
   under these terms.
3. **Third-party code is marked.** Anything you did not write yourself arrives with its own license
   notice intact and is called out in the pull request, so that a copyleft or non-permissive
   component is never merged unnoticed.
4. **Relicensing by the Licensor.** You grant the project's Licensor the right to offer your
   contribution under later versions of the Space Child License published by the Licensor, and
   under the separate written agreements the license provides for (Section 5), on the same
   peace-conditional footing. You keep your copyright.

Sign your commits with the Developer Certificate of Origin (`git commit -s`); the `Signed-off-by`
line is your statement that the four points above are true for that commit.

If you cannot agree to these terms — for example because your employer does not permit it — please
open an issue describing the change instead of submitting the code.

---

*Notes for maintainers.* Point 4 is what lets the Licensor grant a Military Use Agreement covering
contributed code, and move a repository from v1.0 to a later version without chasing every past
contributor. Counsel has not yet confirmed whether this inbound statement is sufficient or whether a
signed CLA is needed (counsel brief, question 9); until then, treat it as the minimum and keep the
contributor list short enough to reach.
